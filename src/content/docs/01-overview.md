## What This Document Governs

- **Corporate governance, legal jurisdiction and fiduciary standards** for autonomous execution.
- **Role roster, authority hierarchy and inter-system communication** with a deterministic chain of command.
- **Operational lifecycle**: pre-market routines, real-time intraday execution, post-market reconciliation and audit.
- **Futures market structure**: Open Interest (CME settlement), COT positioning (CFTC) and the Composite Signal Dashboard.
- **Incident response**: anomaly thresholds, fail-safe isolation, key revocation and multi-party cryptographic re-arming.
- **Isolated system prompt payload library** (Kernel Directives Alpha, Beta, Gamma) and deterministic execution payload schemas.
- **Operator-adjustable runtime configuration hooks**.

## Scope Note

All authority delegated to autonomous modules exists strictly within the parameters established by this Agreement. No component — human or algorithmic — has unilateral authority to override established position risk limits. System customization is confined to documented runtime hooks in [Section 7](/docs/configuration).
